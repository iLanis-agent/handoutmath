Handout Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts print runs with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the sheets a run of copies takes, the reams, the stacks, and the cost. It uses no named constants. The copies, the pages a copy, the pages a sheet, the sheets a ream, the stack size and the prices are yours to decide and read off your own document, paper and receipt. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your document or printer and does not know about jams, spoilage, test prints or toner, and it gives no verdict that a run is enough or will print correctly. Every number here is your own entry.

The sheets truth

Sheets: the pages a copy divided by the pages a sheet, rounded up, times the copies.

The ream truth

Reams: the sheets divided by the sheets a ream, rounded up, with the sheets spare.

The stack truth

Stacks: the copies divided by the stack size, rounded up, with the size of the last stack.

The cost truth

Cost: the reams and the sheets, each at their price.
