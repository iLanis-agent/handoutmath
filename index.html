Handout Math

The 30 copies of 5 pages at 2 pages a sheet take 3 sheets a copy, 90 sheets in all.

Boundary: arithmetic only Handout Math counts print runs with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the sheets a run of copies takes, the reams, the stacks, and the cost. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your document or printer and does not know about jams, spoilage, test prints or toner, and it gives no verdict that a run is enough or will print correctly. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 30 copies of 5 pages, 2 pages a sheet, reams of 500, stacks of 8, a ream at $7.50 and sheets at $0.04.

The ream truth

The 90 sheets in reams of 500 take 1 ream, leaving 410 sheets spare.

The stack truth

The 30 copies in stacks of 8 make 4 stacks, the last stack holding 6.

The cost truth

The 1 ream at $7.50 and 90 sheets at $0.04 cost $11.10, reams $7.50 and sheets $3.60.
